How a remodel works

Three stages, with clear updates at each one.

Plan and design

We meet to talk through your vision, budget and style, then shape a layout and material list that fits.

Choose your finishes

Cabinets, tile, fixtures, lighting and appliances. We help you pick, so every piece works together.

Build

Construction begins with a clear schedule. We keep you posted from demolition to the final walkthrough.
